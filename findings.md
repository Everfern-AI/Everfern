Navis was attempting to log in to newsdiscordbot.xyz using Discord authentication. The process involved navigating to the site, initiating the login via Discord, and authorizing access. However, the process was interrupted by a verification step required by Discord.

**Data Points Discovered:**

*   **Website:** newsdiscordbot.xyz
*   **Authentication Method:** Discord
*   **Discord OAuth2 URL:** `https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1051878285031788575&response_type=code&redirect_uri=https%3A%2F%2Fnewsdiscordbot.xyz%2Fauth-success&scope=identify%20email%20guilds%20guilds.members.read%20guilds.join`
*   **Required Scopes for Discord Authorization:** `identify`, `email`, `guilds`, `guilds.members.read`, `guilds.join`
*   **Interruption:** A Discord verification step was encountered, preventing the login from completing. The message displayed was: "You need to verify your account in order to perform this action."
*   **Last Action:** The "Close" button on the Discord verification dialog was clicked.
